The Mischief Menu

Drink or Treat!

Cover small cups with white tissue and secure with rubber bands; place candy in some and small drink surprises in others. Mark any alcohol clearly before consumption; use sealed or food-safe containers for liquids. Invite guests to choose a cup and reveal their fate.

Supplies: cups, white tissue, rubber bands, candy, drink options, labels, tray, napkins.

Fork in the Pumpkin

Put a designated pumpkin in a common area. Players try to sneak a blunt plastic fork into it without being spotted. If caught, they take a playful penalty: a sip, silly dare or lost point — never a mandatory shot. Reset the fork after each attempt.

Supplies: pumpkin, blunt plastic fork, rules card.

Dolphin

One player leaves. The group chooses a harmless task (such as touch a particular pillow). The player returns and tries to discover the task while everyone guides them using only dolphin-like noises, louder/warmer as they get closer. No words!

Spicy Spoons

Play standard Spoons with one fewer spoon than players, except hide the spoons in safe, accessible spots around the room before the round. When someone gets four of a kind, they quietly go find a spoon; everyone else follows. No running, climbing or grabbing near breakables.

Supplies: deck of cards and seven spoons for eight players.

Saturday evening

Couples’ Potion-Off

Four couples each prepare a signature cocktail or mocktail, enough for eight small tasting pours. Ask for a magical name, a ridiculous power and a warning label. Keep recipes secret until the reveal.

Judging idea: Taste (1–5), Presentation (1–5), Name & Lore (1–5). Total possible: 15 points per entry.

Set out tasting cups, water, napkins, pens and a scoreboard. Make zero-proof options equally welcome.
